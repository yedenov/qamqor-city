# Five-minute presentation script

## 0:00 — Problem and product

“People often want to help their neighborhood but lack a small, concrete first step. Qamqor City makes initiatives discoverable and turns interest into a personal plan.”

Show the overview. Explain the prototype boundary immediately: example initiatives, local state, no invented live partners or user counts.

## 0:40 — Discovery

Open the initiative catalog, choose ecology, and open “Зелёные выходные”. Point out the time, district, description and preparation checklist. Save the initiative. The saved count is real application state.

## 1:20 — Participation

Add the initiative to the personal plan. Complete a preparation task. Open “Мои дела” and add a short reflection. Refresh once to show persistence.

## 2:00 — Understand progress

Open “Мой вклад”. Explain exactly what the chart and ring count. Planned time is distinguished from measured or verified volunteer hours. The first-step badge is a local milestone, not a certificate.

## 2:40 — Build a local idea

Create “Книжная полка нашего двора”. Enter a specific goal and a date in October 2026. Show live preview and validation. Save it, then find it in the catalog and calendar.

## 3:40 — Supporting journeys

Open the schematic map, select a marker and follow the linked card. Open one of the six guidance articles. Explain how these pages help a new participant make a informed first step.

## 4:20 — Engineering

Show `domain.js` and run `npm test`. Explain idempotent joins, task ownership, derived metrics and allowlisted imports. Export a JSON backup from the profile. Mention the deliberate lack of runtime dependencies.

## 5:00 — Next step

Describe a small, authorized pilot with a real organizer as future work. The next credible milestone is usability feedback and verified event data, followed by authentication and moderation if public participation is needed.

Do not claim the generated prototype proves past field work, real volunteering or real societal outcomes. Record your own research, changes and pilot results as they actually happen.
