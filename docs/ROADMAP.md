# Roadmap toward a real civic service

## 1. Validate the product with people

- Observe a small number of consenting participants finding a suitable task and creating a plan.
- Record actual usability findings, limitations and design changes.
- Review the Russian interface and develop a Kazakh localization with a fluent reviewer.
- Complete keyboard and screen-reader review, including mobile navigation and long-content behavior.

## 2. Pilot with an authorized organizer

- Obtain permission to use real initiative descriptions and dates.
- Model contact, accessibility, eligibility and cancellation details explicitly.
- Distinguish personal intention, submitted registration, accepted participation and attendance.
- Define who can confirm attendance and how disputes are corrected.

## 3. Introduce shared infrastructure

- Add authenticated profiles, durable storage and synchronization.
- Add ownership checks and a moderation workflow for public submissions.
- Define retention, export and deletion policy with users.
- Keep contact information private by default; support abuse reporting.
- Use a real map source only after resolving licensing and verified location data.

## 4. Measure outcomes carefully

- Keep planned time separate from recorded time and verified attendance.
- Agree on outcome measures with organizers; document baselines and uncertainty.
- Publish honest case studies with consent and without fabricated reach or benefits.
- Preserve a public changelog of verified improvements and remaining limitations.

The existing prototype intentionally stops before public coordination. These items are future work, not current product claims.
